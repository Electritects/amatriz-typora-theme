## 3. Documentary Evidence & Provenance Archive
Store documentary evidence and legal artifacts in this folder (`legal/compliance/`):
- **Licenses & Notices**: Third-party license texts, dependency manifests (`package.json`, `Cargo.lock`, `requirements.txt`, etc.), and package audits.
- **Asset Provenance**: Proof of asset provenance (licensed icons, purchased/free fonts, sound effects, visual assets).
- **Store Submissions & Platform Inquiries**: Transcripts of App Store Resolution Center or Google Play inquiries (e.g. copyright/trademark proof, export compliance).
- **Terms & Privacy**: Snapshots of active Terms of Service and Privacy Policies.
